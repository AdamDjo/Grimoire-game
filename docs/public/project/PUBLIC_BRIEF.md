# Public Brief

**GRIMOIRE — Of Ash and Salt** is an AI narrative roguelike set in **Velkhar**, a dark fantasy desert world.

The player explores, makes free-form choices, rolls dice at decisive moments, and leaves traces in a world that remembers. The backend acts as the Game Master: it owns rules, dice, inventory, consequences and memory. The AI writes prose only.

Velkhar is a world of ash, salt and memory. Every run begins at the Auberge de L'Aveugle, a threshold between old lives and the next story.

Public keywords: Velkhar, Cendre, Calamine, Souvenirs, L'Aveugle, Marcheur-du-Sel, Lame-Ombre, Veilleur, Tisse-Verbe.

The complete canon, business plans, prompts and roadmap live in private local docs.
